## What would flip a no

- My university or supervisor saying it's allowed, plus being hard-blocked on transcript analysis right before a deadline with no free alternative.
- Being able to buy just the interview guide check or the transcript coding on its own for around $10-15, or my supervisor recommending it.
- A few classmates in my cohort group saying it actually got their proposal approved by the supervisor faster, or the university or my supervisor recommending it.
- If the free framework turned out clearly better than what I get from ChatGPT, and my supervisor or another PhD student said the methodology chain actually held up, I'd pay when I get to fieldwork. Being able to pay for it from my research budget would make it easy.
- My university or supervisor saying this tool is allowed, for example if it is on an approved list or the supervisor recommends it.
- If my university or department paid for it, or if the transcript analysis alone clearly saved me weeks of coding in NVivo during my data-analysis crunch.
- My supervisor or my university's AI guidance saying it's fine to use, or the tool being on a list my school recommends.
- Seeing that it supports mixed-methods designs in my discipline, and hitting a real wall on transcript coding with a deadline close, would get me to pay, probably at a student discount closer to $25.
- My supervisor or graduate school saying it's fine to use, plus a clear statement on how transcript data is stored, would make me pay for it when I get to the analysis stage.
- Something official from my university or supervisor saying it's allowed, like an approved-tools list or a supervisor sign-off, ideally with a student discount.
- If the free diagram couldn't be exported or used in my proposal without paying, and it clearly supported mixed-methods designs (survey instrument plus interview guide tied to the same framework), I'd pay the $49 right before my proposal deadline.
- If two or three classmates in the group said it got their proposal approved, or my supervisor said it's fine to use, I'd pay for it before the deadline.
