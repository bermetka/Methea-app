## How to review a card
1. Is the core idea correct and fair?
2. Are the development works the ones a specialist would name?
3. Is a major critique missing?
4. Chapter numbers in "must read" (only some cards have them): check against the book.
5. Would a student misread anything?
6. Set `"status": "published"`, fill the human line in `verification_log`.
